## Recommendation

Contact ranks 1–3 first. The Transit can become the clear winner only if it is a
high-roof body with at least 1,900 mm of finished height. The Delica is the
functional favorite for difficult Georgian roads, but its age and mileage make a
pre-purchase inspection essential. The Quest is the strongest low-friction road
minivan because it already publishes a VIN that matches the advertised identity.

The Caravan should be the next contact if one of the first three fails. It could
offer the best space per lari, but its period of inactivity and absent evidence
raise the current risk.

## Evidence notes

- A clean NHTSA VIN decode confirms encoded identity and check digit; it does not
  confirm accident, mileage, ownership, repair or mechanical history.
- Standard minivans cannot meet the 1.9 m standing-height preference. Exact bed
  length must be measured behind the driver's normal driving position.
- The current ranking is intentionally confidence-aware. Verified dimensions,
  VINs and inspection results should trigger a full replay and reranking.
