# Initial vehicle criteria — v1

## Ranked strategies

1. A van with at least 2,000 mm of usable sleeping length and preferably at
   least 1,900 mm of finished interior height. Volkswagen Crafter is now an
   explicit sourcing target in this strategy; roof height and usable cargo
   dimensions still require listing-specific evidence.
2. A sleepable all-wheel-drive van or minivan, initially represented by the
   Mitsubishi Delica family.
3. A spacious road-oriented minivan, initially including Toyota Voxy, Nissan
   Elgrand, Honda Odyssey, Nissan Caravan, Toyota Noah, and Honda Stepwgn.
4. A small, reliable, easy-to-resell car that can carry a tent and camping gear.

## Firm and provisional requirements

## Customs-cost presentation

For a vehicle physically in Georgia but not customs-cleared, show:

1. the advertised purchase price;
2. estimated excise tax;
3. estimated import duty and separately identified administrative fees;
4. the estimated customs-inclusive total;
5. assumptions for production year, engine displacement, steering side, hybrid
   status and passenger/commercial customs classification.

Use the current Revenue Service calculator or current Tax Code. Do not present a
single-point total when classification or steering side is unknown; show a range
or mark the estimate blocked. Vehicles outside Georgia are rejected before this
calculation because shipping time is a hard blocker.

## Ranking rule for the budget

Until concrete price ceilings are provided, do not collapse everything into one
score. Show a Pareto frontier across:

- functional fit;
- acquisition price and customs exposure;
- expected repair/maintenance burden;
- evidence completeness and risk.

This prevents a cheap small car from outranking a genuinely useful van solely on
price, while still making the premium for each extra capability visible.
