# Initial vehicle criteria — v1

## Ranked strategies

1. A van with at least 2,000 mm of usable sleeping length and preferably at
   least 1,900 mm of finished interior height.
2. A sleepable all-wheel-drive van or minivan, initially represented by the
   Mitsubishi Delica family.
3. A spacious road-oriented minivan, initially including Toyota Voxy, Nissan
   Elgrand, Honda Odyssey, Nissan Caravan, Toyota Noah, and Honda Stepwgn.
4. A small, reliable, easy-to-resell car that can carry a tent and camping gear.

## Firm and provisional requirements

| Criterion                           | Current treatment               | Notes                                                                                               |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Usable sleeping length ≥ 2,000 mm   | Hard for strategies 1–3         | Must be measured behind the driver's normal seating position, not inferred from total cabin length. |
| Finished interior height ≥ 1,900 mm | Preference                      | Requires a genuine high-roof cargo van; ordinary minivans do not satisfy it.                        |
| Georgia usability                   | Preference                      | Distinguish paved-road access, winter traction, and genuine rough-road capability.                  |
| Reliability                         | Preference                      | Must be evaluated by generation, engine, transmission, and maintenance evidence.                    |
| Resale liquidity in Georgia         | Preference                      | To be estimated from local listing volume, price dispersion, parts, and market evidence.            |
| Purchase and total budget           | Unknown                         | Budget rises with functional fit; use a Pareto comparison until explicit ceilings are supplied.     |
| VIN and identity consistency        | Required before qualification   | No candidate is fully qualified without adequate identity evidence.                                 |
| Inspection/test drive in Tbilisi    | Required before finalist status | Seller confirmation remains missing until messaging is enabled.                                     |

## Ranking rule for the budget

Until concrete price ceilings are provided, do not collapse everything into one
score. Show a Pareto frontier across:

- functional fit;
- acquisition price and customs exposure;
- expected repair/maintenance burden;
- evidence completeness and risk.

This prevents a cheap small car from outranking a genuinely useful van solely on
price, while still making the premium for each extra capability visible.
