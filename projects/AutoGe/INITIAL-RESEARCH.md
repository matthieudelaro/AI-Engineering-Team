# Initial research — 2026-08-06

This is an early market pass, not a purchase recommendation. No seller was
contacted. Listing claims remain unverified until supported by VIN, inspection,
documents, and independent history.

## Main conclusion

The 1,900 mm standing-height preference divides the search into two genuinely
different markets:

- normal minivans such as Delica, Voxy, Elgrand, Odyssey, and Caravan can be
  sleepable but cannot provide standing height;
- standing height requires a verified high-roof commercial van.

Mitsubishi publishes 2,990 mm of total interior length, 1,505 mm of width, and
1,310 mm of height for the Delica D:5. The total length does **not** prove a
2,000 mm bed behind the driver's normal position, but the 1,310 mm height proves
that standing is impossible in an unmodified D:5. The live Delica found on
auto.ge is an older 2000 model, so D:5 dimensions cannot be applied to it.

Toyota's first-generation Voxy specification gives 2,680 mm of interior length
and 1,340 mm of interior height. It may support a bed layout, but not standing.
Each generation and seat layout still requires separate measurement.

A high-roof 2018 Sprinter has a manufacturer-listed maximum cargo height of
75.4 inches with trim, approximately 1,915 mm. The standard-roof 2018 Sprinter
found on auto.ge is listed explicitly as `STANDARD ROOF`, for which the same
brochure lists 64 inches, approximately 1,626 mm. That live vehicle therefore
does not meet the standing-height preference.

## Data-quality warnings found immediately

- auto.ge's keyword search behaves broadly and can return unrelated models;
- listing equipment fields may be seller-entered defaults or inconsistent;
- the Delica record marks `Turbo: Yes` despite a stated 3.0 petrol/LPG engine;
- the Sprinter record calls its 3.0 engine `Gas` and `Turbo: No`, which conflicts
  with common 2018 Sprinter 2500 V6 configurations and must not be trusted;
- unusually round or low mileages need documentary evidence;
- `Customs Passed` and `Before Customs` materially change total cost.
